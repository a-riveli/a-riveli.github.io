# Support

We're here to help! If you have any questions, suggestions, or concerns about our apps, please don't hesitate to reach out.

## Contact Information

**Email:** info@riveli.io

## How Can We Help?

We welcome:
- Questions about our apps
- Feature requests and suggestions
- Bug reports
- Business cooperation inquiries
- Development partnership opportunities

We strive to respond to all inquiries within 24-48 business hours.

## Before Contacting Support

For app-specific issues, please include:
- App name
- Your device model
- Operating system version
- Clear description of the issue

This information helps us assist you more effectively.

Thank you for using our apps!
